# Evaluation Plan

Phase 1 contains **no evaluation results** and no fabricated metrics. Future evaluation will use versioned synthetic scenarios with known outcomes and compare deterministic baselines against agent-assisted decisions.

Planned metrics include recovered value, avoided duplicate actions, policy violations, escalation rate, uncertain outcomes, latency, malformed-output rate, and audit completeness. Results will be segmented by failure type and reported with scenario counts, assumptions, and limitations.
