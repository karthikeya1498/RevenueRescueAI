# Safety and Policy

The LLM is not the final authority because it can be wrong, stale, malformed, or manipulated by context. Deterministic policy must approve every financial or customer-impacting action.

Future policy includes maximum retries, duplicate protection, success protection, permitted action enumeration, escalation thresholds, customer-contact constraints, stop conditions, and append-only audit requirements. Tools receive validated typed inputs and least-privilege credentials. A policy rejection is a safe, explainable stop—not an invitation for the agent to improvise.
